# ExampleBank: service facts (fictional bank, invented for this project)

- Accounts: for residents of EU/EEA countries, age 18+. Identity check in the app: photo ID + selfie, usually approved within 1 business day. We may also ask where large deposits come from (source of funds).
- Cards: Visa or Mastercard (customer chooses). Physical card delivery 5-7 business days, express 2 business days for €15. First card free, replacement or spare card €5. Activate a new card in the app under Cards > Activate.
- Virtual cards: free, created instantly in the app. Disposable virtual cards work for one payment, then get a new number.
- PIN: change in the app under Cards > PIN or at any ATM. After 3 wrong attempts the PIN is blocked; unblock it in the app under Cards > Unblock PIN.
- Apple Pay and Google Pay are supported. Contactless limit €50 per payment.
- Top-ups: by bank transfer free (arrives in 1-2 business days); by card 0.5% fee (usually instant, max 1 hour). Cash and cheque top-ups are not supported. Limits: €5,000 per top-up, €20,000 per month. A failed or reverted card top-up is returned to the card within 5 business days.
- Transfers: SEPA transfers in euros arrive in 1 business day, instant transfers within seconds. International (non-SEPA) transfers take 2-5 business days, fee €5. A transfer can be cancelled only while it is pending.
- Card payments: pending payments usually complete within 7 days. A reverted payment means the merchant cancelled it; the money returns in 3-7 days. Merchant refunds take 5-10 business days to appear.
- Currency: exchange in the app at 0.5% on weekdays, 1% on weekends. Card payments and ATM withdrawals in other currencies use the Visa/Mastercard rate + 0.5%.
- Cash withdrawals: free up to €200 per month, then 2% (min €2).
- All fees are listed in the app under Account > Fees.
